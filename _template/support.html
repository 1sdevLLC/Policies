{{#if has_subscriptions}}
How do I restore my purchases?

Open the app, go to Settings (or the paywall screen), and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy. If it still doesn't work, email us with your Apple receipt or order number.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose the subscription, and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also manage subscriptions from the App Store app (tap your profile picture → Subscriptions). Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. You'll keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and choose the purchase you'd like to report. We can't issue refunds directly because Apple processes all payments, but we're happy to help if you run into trouble.

I was charged but my purchase isn't unlocked.

First try Restore Purchases in the app. Then force-quit and reopen the app with an internet connection. If the problem continues, email us your Apple order number and we'll investigate.
