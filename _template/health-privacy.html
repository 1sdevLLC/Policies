2. Sources of consumer health data

Directly from you (data you enter), from Apple Health with your permission, and from your device's sensors or connected devices you authorize. We do not obtain consumer health data about you from data brokers.

3. How we use it

We use consumer health data only to provide the features you request, as described above. We do not use it for advertising or marketing, do not sell it, and do not use it for data mining or to build advertising profiles. We do not store HealthKit data in iCloud.

4. Sharing with third parties

{{HEALTH_THIRD_PARTIES}}

We share consumer health data with a third party or affiliate only with your separate consent (or as needed to provide a service you request), or as required by law. We do not "sell" consumer health data. Service providers that process data for us do so under contract and only on our instructions.

5. Geofencing

We do not use a geofence around any facility that provides in-person health care to identify or track you or to send you health-related messages.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can:

  • confirm whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data, and access it (including a list of any third parties and affiliates it was shared with);
  • withdraw consent to collection or sharing at any time;
  • delete your consumer health data (we will delete it from our records and notify service providers to do the same, within the time required by law); and
  • correct inaccurate data or receive a portable copy.

How to make a request: use the controls in the App, or email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. We'll respond within 45 days. We won't discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Appeals: if we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our response with the subject line "Appeal." We'll respond within 45 days with a written explanation. If your appeal is denied, you may contact your state attorney general (in Washington, at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint).

7. Security and retention

We protect consumer health data with safeguards including encryption in transit and on device, restricted access, and minimizing what we collect and store. We keep consumer health data only as long as needed to provide the features you requested, or until you delete it or withdraw consent, unless we are required by law to keep it longer.

8. Changes and contact

We'll post any changes here and update the "Last updated" date; for material changes affecting how we collect or share consumer health data, we'll ask for your consent again where required.
